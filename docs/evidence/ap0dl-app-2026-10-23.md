## Notes

- AnimeToki remains labelled **experimental** because AT-Cloud enforces per-file
  download quotas; the app reports the quota error instead of a generic network
  failure.
- AniSkip is only available when a provider supplies a MAL id. The fallback is
  intentionally visible during the first 95 seconds of an episode.
- 16:9 video is letterboxed on the 20:9 emulator display; the player preserves
  the source aspect ratio rather than cropping.
- The debug-signed release is for sideloading. A production Play signing key was
  not part of this goal.
- The phone was disconnected during the final pass, so the final APK was copied
  to `C:\Users\rares\Downloads\AP0DL-1.1.0.apk` for manual installation. The
  emulator install of the same binary succeeded.
